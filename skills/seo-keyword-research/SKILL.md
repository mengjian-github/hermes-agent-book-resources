# SEO Keyword Research

Use this skill when the user asks to research keywords, search intent, competitor pages, or content gaps.

## Workflow

1. Clarify target market, language, seed keywords, and business goal.
2. Use web search or provided sources to identify search intent and competitor pages.
3. Extract page titles, headings, positioning, and content gaps.
4. Group keywords by intent: informational, commercial, navigational, or transactional.
5. Summarize opportunities, risks, and recommended content angles.

## Output Format

- Keyword groups
- Search intent
- Competitor observations
- Content gaps
- Recommended article or landing page angles

## Verification

Check that every conclusion is tied to a source page or an explicit assumption.
